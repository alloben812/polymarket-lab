## How to read the columns

- **gross** = `trade_pnl` = the "Profit/Loss" number shown on the polymarket.com profile (before fees). This is the number every post quotes.
- **fees paid** = `fees_paid` = fees charged minus `fees_refunded`. Taker fees charged in Jan-Apr 2026 were partly refunded: across the 72 wallets `fees_refunded` is 109K in Jan, 90K in Feb,
  959K in Mar, 1,781K in Apr 2026 and zero from May 2026 on (e.g. Bonereaper: charged 2,524K, refunded 907K, paid 1,617K).
  (`tools/wallet_check.py` was updated during the day to show refunds; the numbers below were computed independently from `fees_paid` and `economic_pnl`.)
- **rebates** = taker_rebate + maker_rebate + reward_income + referral_income.
- **NET** = `economic_pnl` (what was kept). **net w/o reb** = NET minus rebates = what a new account without a volume tier would have kept from the same trades.
- **reb share** = rebates / NET. Over 100% means the trading itself lost money after fees and the account lives on rebates.
- **monthly** = Jun / Jul / Aug / Sep / Oct / Nov 2026 (Nov is 1-27), in $K: first NET, then net without rebates.
- **markets** = `trades` field of user-stats (number of markets traded).
- Wallet names were read from the video poster of each post (downloaded, cropped, read by eye) when the text did not give them; every name was resolved through
  gamma `public-search` and the resolved address matches the address fragment printed on the poster.
